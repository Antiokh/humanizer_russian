## Overgeneralization audit — additional integration corrections

The previous audit already narrows all 38 rules. This re-audit adds implementation-specific restrictions:

1. **R09 abbreviation effort**: acronym length/density can only surface a candidate; actual reader effort depends on audience and first-use context.
2. **R15 register leakage**: generic business/technical phrases such as `в рамках проекта` or `проводится проверка` are not enough for a bureaucratic-register verdict.
3. **R17 nominalization**: suffix counting cannot establish cancelearite. It may only surface an event-reconstruction candidate.
4. **R18 modifier subtraction**: lexical candidates are examples for an A/B semantic test, never a stop-list.
5. **R19 templates**: a phrase list cannot establish a dead stamp; repeated discourse function is the real criterion.
6. **R22/R31 prosody**: suffix/ending echo is descriptive data. It must be `METRIC_ONLY`, not an automatic editing suggestion.
7. **R23 dependency ambiguity**: do not simulate syntactic/semantic role reconstruction with a regex case counter. Keep model/dependency-parser work separate.
8. **R29 fresh-vs-lexicalized semantics**: antonymic abstract pairs such as `наличие/отсутствие` are not evidence of semantic collision. The old `ABSTRACT_COLLISIONS` regex is rejected.
9. **R24 metadiscourse**: a surface match may trigger only an A/B deletion test; it is not evidence of AI authorship.
10. **R25 `вопрос` packaging**: only repeated procedural-shell patterns merit a soft candidate; a genuine issue/topic remains valid.
