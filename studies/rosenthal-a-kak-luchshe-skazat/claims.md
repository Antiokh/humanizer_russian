# Claims requiring current verification

The 1979 source contains large inventories of noun gender/case variants, numeral forms, agreement patterns, government, pronunciation and stress. They are retained as source-period evidence, not copied into current NORM.

Current verification completed for runtime-sensitive claims:

- double comparative marking (`более лучше`, `более веселее`) — `CURRENT_CONFIRMED` as improper in neutral/literary use when accidental; intentional metalinguistic or playful use is excluded;
- duplicated `бы` inside one subordinate subjunctive predication (`чтобы … получил бы`, `если бы … был бы`) — `CURRENT_CONFIRMED` as a problem, but `Что было бы, если бы…` is correct because the particles belong to different predications.
