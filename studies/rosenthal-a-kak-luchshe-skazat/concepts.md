# Concepts

1. **Norm vs choice.** Mandatory correctness and stylistic choice are different decision layers.
2. **Diachrony.** A rulebook is evidence for its period; current NORM needs current verification.
3. **Functional styles.** Coexisting variants may differ by register rather than correctness.
4. **Stylistic synonymy.** Lexical, morphological and syntactic alternatives may express similar content with different focus, tone or rhythm.
5. **Multi-criterion editing.** Precision, clarity, logic, economy, naturalness and expressiveness are jointly optimized; no one metric is the objective function.
6. **Expressive syntax is native capacity.** Inversion, ellipsis, parceling and rhetorical figures are resources, not automatic defects.
