## Normative-age risk

All books predate ГОСТ Р 7.0.97-2025. Therefore:

- document layout;
- mandatory requisites;
- exact forms of dates/addresses/signatures;
- references to former ГОСТ editions;
- procedural-form claims tied to then-current legislation

must be externally revalidated before becoming current mechanical rules.

## Potential overgeneralizations to prevent

### Direct word order

Several teaching materials describe direct word order as characteristic of official-business style. This is a stylistic tendency, not a universal Russian syntax law. The checker must not flag ordinary marked Russian word order unless it creates ambiguity or register mismatch.

### Short sentences

Some materials recommend shorter sentences and avoiding nested subordination. Do not turn sentence length into an error metric. Legal syntax may legitimately be long when dependencies remain unambiguous.

### Impersonal style

Impersonality is common in official documents, but not universally desirable. Contractual obligations usually need explicit party attribution; procedural documents may require first-person statements.

### Clichés

Conventional formulae are often functional. However, source approval of clichés does not protect meaningless padding or archaic formulas.

### Emotiveness

Neutrality is a strong prior for normative, contractual and administrative documents, but complaints, petitions, claims and advocacy documents may contain controlled evaluative or emotional language. Genre matters.
