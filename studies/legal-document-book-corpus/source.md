## Source-status policy

These books are scholarship/training sources, not current positive law. Their language and composition observations can support `DOMAIN_CONVENTION`, `STYLE_WARNING`, `PROJECT_DERIVED` and genre heuristics.

Any claim about mandatory requisites, forms, ГОСТ requirements or procedural validity must be checked against the active normative layer before activation.

Older formatting guidance is not promoted to `CURRENT_NORM` merely because several books repeat it.

## Stable locators used in first extraction

- Марьева: ч. 2, гл. 7, pp. 120–122 — stages of document creation; genre and composition; final audit.
- Абрамова: section 3.3, pp. 129–131 — features and general rules of legal writing.
- Новосельцева: pp. 7–8 — official-business style and clichés; pp. 73–74 — editing sequence and common defects.
- Беглова: p. 144 onward — language design of document types and directive modality.
- Мотякина/Лопатин: around pp. 20–21 — requirements of legal technique.
- Михайлова: section 8 and exercises around pp. 54–56 — editing/error corpus for eval design.
