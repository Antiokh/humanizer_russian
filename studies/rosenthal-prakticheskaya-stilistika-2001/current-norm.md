# Current-norm audit

The source is a 2001 practical-stylistics handbook. Current normative claims are not inherited from author authority.

## Verified boundaries

### Geographic names on `-ово/-ево/-ино/-ыно`

The source's §77 distinguishes a name used with a generic noun from the bare toponym. Its example `в городе Кемерово` is compatible with current guidance: with a generic noun, the name normally remains unchanged. For the bare toponym, current strict literary norm requires declension: `в Кемерове`.

Current evidence:
- https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/332830
- https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=all&query=%D0%BA%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BE

Status: **CURRENT_CONFIRMED with structural boundary**. Do not turn the source example into a blanket `Кемерово`-is-indeclinable rule.

### `кофе`

§22 belongs to a broad inventory of gender behavior for indeclinable nouns. Current guidance keeps masculine as the standard literary gender and permits neuter agreement in colloquial speech.

Current evidence:
- https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/100006

Status: **SOURCE_PERIOD inventory + CURRENT_CONFIRMED register boundary**.

### Comparative forms

§37 explicitly rejects combinations such as `более интереснее` / `более лучше`, but treats `более старший` separately. Current Gramota guidance confirms that `более старший возраст` is acceptable.

Current evidence:
- https://gramota.ru/biblioteka/spravochniki/russkij-yazyk-kratkij-teoreticheskij-kurs-dlya-shkolnikov/stepeni-sravneniya-prilagatelnykh
- https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/324406

Status: **CURRENT_CONFIRMED**. `ROS-R53` must remain whitelist/boundary driven and must not flag `более старший`.

### `согласно`

§80 itself marks genitive constructions like `согласно приказа` as non-current and contrasts them with `согласно приказу`.

Current evidence:
- https://gramota.ru/biblioteka/spravochniki/slovar-trudnostey/soglasno

Status: **CURRENT_CONFIRMED**; reinforces `ROS-R44`.

### Payment verbs

§82 contrasts `уплатить за проезд` with `оплатить проезд` and notes spread of `оплатить за` in colloquial/official speech. Current guidance retains the normative distinction `оплатить проезд` / `заплатить за проезд`.

Current evidence:
- https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/208929
- https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/256269

Status: **CURRENT_CONFIRMED**; reinforces `ROS-R45`.
