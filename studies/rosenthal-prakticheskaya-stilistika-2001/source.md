## Source framing

The introduction defines practical stylistics as functional selection among lexical and grammatical variants, with literary norm as a boundary and with meaning/register/function determining the preferred variant. This source is dated 2001: concrete morphological and government lists are evidence of that edition, not automatic 2026 norm.

The book is structured into four parts and 125 numbered paragraphs:
1. language styles (§§1–6);
2. lexical stylistics (§§7–20);
3. grammatical stylistics (§§21–105);
4. expressive means (§§106–125).
