# Interactions — Lynn Visson

1. `VISSON-NATIVE-ASPECT-EVENT-CONSTRUAL` is a prerequisite for `VISSON-CALQUE-PROGRESSIVE-IMPERFECTIVE`; lexical aspect and event class must be diagnosed before choosing a Russian form.
2. `VISSON-CALQUE-BROAD-LEXICAL-MAPPING` is an umbrella diagnostic, not a second warning when a narrower phenomenon already fires. In Board it provides context; Compact should prefer the narrow rule.
3. `VISSON-NATIVE-WORD-ORDER-INFO`, `VISSON-NATIVE-SUBJECT-OMISSION`, `VISSON-NATIVE-IMPERSONAL-DATIVE`, `VISSON-NATIVE-REFLEXIVE-STATE` and `VISSON-CALQUE-SVO-LOCK` must be optimized jointly. Removing a subject can alter reference; changing word order can alter focus.
4. `VISSON-NATIVE-ACTIVE-PASSIVE` interacts with agent salience and cannot be decided from passive morphology alone.
5. `VISSON-NATIVE-MODAL-NEGATION`, `VISSON-NORM-NEGATIVE-CONCORD` and `VISSON-NATIVE-NEGATED-INFINITIVE` all contain surface negation but encode different semantics. Never merge them into a generic “too much negation” rule.
6. Speech-formula rules (`VISSON-EDIT-FORMULA-REGISTER`, `VISSON-CALQUE-HAVE-NICE-DAY`, `VISSON-CALQUE-HAPPY-BIRTHDAY`, `VISSON-CALQUE-ENJOY-STANDALONE`) depend on speech act and register. The three surface rules remain `REVIEW`, not forced replacement.
7. `VISSON-CALQUE-MEETING-MITING` overlaps `russian.register_jargon_or_term`: workplace `митинг` can be intentional jargon and should not be silently normalized.
8. `VISSON-CALQUE-PROGRESSIVE-IMPERFECTIVE` relates to existing `russian.break_state_transition` / `russian.abstract_break_calque` but does not share their provenance.
9. `VISSON-NATIVE-POSSESSIVE-OMISSION` is related to native context economy; preserve explicit possession when contrast or coreference requires it.
10. Cultural claims never vote in Editorial Board as if they were grammar rules. They may explain why Visson chose an English recommendation, but the Russian repair is governed by current semantics, norm, native usage and author intent.
