# Interactions

- Redundancy rules must be evaluated together with `native.functional_repetition` and `native.recoverable_ellipsis`: first identify function, then delete only material with no independent function.
- `editing.information_focus_fit`, pronoun/reference rules and inversion must be optimized jointly; a reorder that fixes one ambiguity can destroy intended theme/rheme.
- `norm.gerund_subject_attachment` and `norm.participle_head_attachment` are hard semantic constraints, while stylistic density/placement remains lower-priority.
- `editing.action_hidden_in_nominalization`, `editing.nominal_chain_obscures_relations` and passive/agent visibility interact; do not force an active verb if the nominalization is a term or the agent is genuinely unknown.
- Register rules never override `USER_INTENT + SEMANTICS + NORM`; dialect, jargon, bookishness and professionalism are conditional choices.
- Image rules are subordinate to intentional figurative meaning. Mixed metaphor and idiom-contamination findings require an accidental reading, not merely novelty.
