# Current-norm audit

The source repeatedly relies on the 1956 orthography/punctuation code, so list-like spelling, capitalization, proper-name transcription and mandatory punctuation prescriptions are not promoted wholesale to current NORM. Current academic/Gramota sources win on concrete present-day prescriptions.

Verified boundaries:

- Authorial punctuation: Gramota answer №314903 defines it as a conscious departure from current punctuation norms serving artistic intent, rhythm or expressiveness; an ordinary mistake is not made correct by calling it authorial. https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/314903
- Same-form verbs: Gramota search/answers preserve `Пойду посмотрю новый фильм` without a comma when the verbs form one semantic unit. https://gramota.ru/poisk?query=%D0%BF%D0%BE%D1%81%D0%BC%D0%BE%D1%82%D1%80%D1%8E
- Different actors: Gramota answer №306315 requires a comma in `пойдём, посмотришь` because the predicates belong to different actors. https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/306315
