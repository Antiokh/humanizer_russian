# Concepts

1. Orthography seeks substantially stronger unification than punctuation.
2. Punctuation signs are multifunctional and may be near-synonymous without being identical.
3. Facultative, alternative and variative punctuation are different decision types.
4. Authorial punctuation is a functional system, not a blanket excuse for mistakes.
5. Punctuation and pause/intonation are related but not isomorphic.
6. Conversational syntax needs its own structural analysis instead of forced bookish normalization.
7. Same-form verb sequences may form one semantic unit (`пойду посмотрю`) and therefore are not automatically comma-separated.
8. Historical spelling/proper-name/capitalization inventories require current verification.
