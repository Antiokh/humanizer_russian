# Interactions

- `ROS-R75` extends `ROS-R65` (logical relation fit), `ROS-R56` (sentence boundary) and `ROS-R74` (pause/information structure) without duplicating them.
- `ROS-R76` intersects `ROS-R55`/`ROS-R58` and the project's preservation of authorial voice.
- `ROS-R77` intersects `ROS-R17`, `ROS-R55`, `ROS-R56`, `ROS-R73`, but is specifically about written punctuation of spoken syntax.
- `ROS-R78` is source-specific evidence for a current Russian construction not represented by an existing Rosenthal identity. Current normative verification remains external to the historical source.
- Concrete orthography and mandatory punctuation are routed to `russian/NORM`, not to new author-specific regexes.
