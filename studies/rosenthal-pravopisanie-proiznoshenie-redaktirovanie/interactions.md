# Interactions and deduplication

Cycle 3 enriches 56 existing Rosenthal rules and adds 15 new source-specific rule IDs. It does not fork shared mechanisms merely because the source is another book.

Important cross-school identities / relationships:

- `editing.local_change_whole_fit` is shared with Nora Gal (`GAL-WHOLE-BEFORE-DETAIL`): both reject locally attractive edits that damage the whole.
- `editing.terminology_audience_fit` is source-neutral and belongs with the existing audience/terminology logic rather than a Rosenthal-only concept.
- `editing.sentence_boundary_function` (`ROS-R56`) is reinforced by §§217 and 220 and remains aligned with Gal/native protection of functional parceling.
- `native.expressive_redundancy` / `ROS-R09` is reinforced by §219: repetition can create cohesion, rhythm and composition.
- `editing.word_order_information_structure` / `ROS-R17` is reinforced at discourse level by §§181–182 and oral logical stress in §243.
- `editing.rhetorical_figure_function` / `ROS-R58` absorbs most of §§219–222; enumerating every named figure as an independent defect would be destructive duplication.
- `norm.orthoepy_requires_current_verification` / `ROS-R59` owns §§235–241. Individual 1999 pronunciations are evidence, not frozen NORM.
- `ai_calque.false_friend_semantic_transfer` (`ROS-R60`) overlaps contrastive/false-friend work such as Visson but keeps book-specific provenance and current-meaning guards.

No unresolved `SOURCE_CONFLICT` requiring a winner was found. Apparent tensions around bureaucracy, borrowing, repetition and rhetorical devices resolve through register/function guards. The genuine historical disagreements are recorded in `current-norm.md` as status changes, not as author-vs-author votes.
