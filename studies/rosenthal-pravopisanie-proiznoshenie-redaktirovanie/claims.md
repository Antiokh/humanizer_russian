# Claims / dispositions

- **Claim:** word choice is simultaneously semantic, situational/register-sensitive and expressive. **Disposition:** operational; enrich existing lexical/register rules.
- **Claim:** a text has supra-sentence organization, microthemes and inter-sentence links. **Disposition:** operational MODEL_ONLY; new text-level rules.
- **Claim:** paragraphing affects meaning and emphasis. **Disposition:** operational MODEL_ONLY; preserve authorial paragraphing unless there is a diagnosed cohesion/composition problem.
- **Claim:** logical relations must be tested independently of conjunctions and punctuation. **Disposition:** operational MODEL_ONLY; no pseudo-semantic regex.
- **Claim:** editing should proceed from large units to smaller ones and every local edit should be reread in context. **Disposition:** workflow/model rule, not user-visible blanket finding.
- **Claim:** rhetorical figures, parceling, ellipsis and repetition are legitimate expressive tools. **Disposition:** preservation guard; enrich existing rules.
- **Claim:** pronunciation/stress lists in this edition are modern norm. **Disposition:** rejected as a blanket inference; source is 1999 and individual forms need current dictionaries.
- **Claim:** positive `амбициозный` is an erroneous English semantic transfer. **Disposition:** obsolete as a blanket claim; contemporary dictionary records a success-oriented meaning.
- **Claim:** `прачечная` must have [шн]. **Disposition:** obsolete as categorical; contemporary norm allows [чн] and [шн].
