# Concepts

Cycle 3 changes the level of analysis more than it changes the surface rule set.

- **Sentence → text:** a sentence is evaluated inside a microtheme and an inter-sentence information chain.
- **Paragraph as operation:** paragraph boundaries alter semantic weight, perspective, rhythm and processing load.
- **Logic before connector:** `поэтому`, `так как`, `а`, a colon or dash do not create a logical relation; the relation must exist in the propositions first.
- **Audience as constraint:** terms, explanations and rhetorical devices are evaluated against reader preparation.
- **Edit as transformation with regression risk:** compare the edited phrase both with the original and with its new context.
- **Author preservation:** neutralization can require compensation elsewhere; reduction is not automatically improvement.
- **Written vs spoken:** pronunciation, pause, intonation and logical stress are a different surface from written orthography/punctuation.
- **Historical lexicon:** even a 1999 semantic warning can age; contemporary dictionaries outrank frozen examples.
