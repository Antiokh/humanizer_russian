## Edition relation

Cycle 4 is chronologically older and narrower than the already integrated 1999 coauthored 3rd edition «Справочник по правописанию, произношению, литературному редактированию». This 1997 book ends at §213 plus an index. The 1999 source restructures the lexical/editorial beginning and adds §§214–243 on text-level editing, rhetorical figures, editorial method and pronunciation. Therefore cycle 4 is treated mainly as independent provenance for mechanisms already present in the cumulative library, not as a reason to create duplicate rules.
