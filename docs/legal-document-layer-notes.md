# Design notes

The legal-document layer is intentionally additive. It must not weaken the existing priorities `SEMANTICS`, `NORM`, native Russian and author voice.

A professional register changes the interpretation of some existing heuristics:

- repeated defined terms may be desirable;
- nominalizations may carry exact domain meaning;
- passive voice may be functionally justified;
- formulaic phrases may be conventional rather than AI markers;
- document-level ambiguity can be more serious than sentence-level heaviness.

Therefore legal-document handling should be a profile that changes rule activation/priority, not a global style rewrite.
